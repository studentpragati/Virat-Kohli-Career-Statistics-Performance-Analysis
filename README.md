## Virat Kohli Career Statistics & Performance Analysis

## Overview
Cricket analysts and fans had no consolidated visual summary of Virat Kohli's international career performance — making it difficult to track his run-scoring trends over time, identify strongest opponents, and benchmark key career milestones like centuries, fifties, and batting average across 516 matches.

## Tools Used
Power BI, DAX, Cricket statistics dataset

## What I Did
Sourced and modeled Virat Kohli's career batting data spanning 2008–2022 across international matches
Built 6 KPI cards displaying Sum of 50s (129), 100s (77), Total Runs (24K), Highest Score (254), Batting Average % (45.95), and Team Count (516)
Created a bar chart for runs by opponent — comparing performance against Australia, England, West Indies, Sri Lanka, and South Africa
Designed a donut chart for count of innings by opponent — England (103), Australia (95), Sri Lanka (73), West Indies (70), South Africa (61)
Built an area chart showing sum of runs by year (2008–2022) to visualize career growth and peak periods
Applied a branded dashboard design using a Virat Kohli action image background with "King Kohli" typography for visual impact

## Key Insights
Kohli has scored 24K total runs across 516 matches with a career average of 45.95
Australia is the opponent against whom he has scored the most runs — his most productive rival
England leads in innings count (103) — Kohli has faced England more than any other team
Career peak was 2016–2018 — consistently touching 2.5K–3K runs per year in that window
Significant run dip post-2020 visible in the area chart — reflects form/availability decline
77 international centuries and 129 half-centuries confirm elite consistency across formats
Highest score of 254 reflects his Test batting prowess alongside his ODI dominance

## Impact
Created a compelling data-driven tribute and analysis tool that allows cricket analysts, fans, and selectors to quickly assess career patterns, identify peak years, and compare opponent-wise performance — all in a single visually branded Power BI page.

## Dashboard Output
Single-page Power BI dashboard with branded Virat Kohli background image, 6 KPI cards, runs by opponent bar chart, innings count by opponent donut chart, and career runs by year area chart (2008–2022).
